### Reflection
---
Participating in the industrial visit to Johor Port was a truly eye-opening experience that brought our classroom theories to life. I was genuinely amazed during the port tour as we stood in the shadow of the massive container ships as seeing the sheer scale of these vessels involved in international trade made me realize the gravity of the operations we were studying. Guided by Mr. Mohamad Taufik and Mr. Badrul Hisham, we learned how this physical magnitude is managed through sophisticated Terminal Operating Systems (TOS), low-code platforms, and robust IT infrastructures like Metrocluster. I was particularly struck by the critical need for system reliability, understanding that in such a high-stakes environment, even minor IT downtime could lead to massive financial losses. This visit broadened my perspective, showing me that a Computer Science degree can lead to a vital career at the intersection of technology and global logistics, where code and data directly drive the efficiency of the world's supply chains.
